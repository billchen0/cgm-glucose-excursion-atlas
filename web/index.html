Required measurement bundle for a future benchmark

Measurement recommendations from the evidence; they do not claim that AI-READI analysis is authorized.

  1. Freeze the event definition and external reference standard.
  2. Use participant-level splits and report internal versus external validation.
  3. Report recall, precision, F1, and false positives per person-day together.
  4. Report signed and absolute timing error, with distribution/interval.
  5. Predeclare event-matching tolerance and one-to-one event matching.
  6. Report performance by person and aggregate without hiding failure cases.
  7. Include calibration/abstention and explicit unmatched/missed-event handling where probabilistic models permit it.
  8. Keep response-characterization agreement separate from event-detection performance.

synthesis Characterization has many metrics but limited event-boundary consensus. Phenotyping is methodologically diverse but replication-light. Biological value is mostly cross-sectional.

Evidence overview

Bottom line. synthesis CGM-only event detection is technically established but not yet portable: the strongest shared benchmark found large trade-offs among sensitivity, false detections, and latency in only 16 healthy adults.[6] Phenotyping methods span hand-crafted windows, functional data analysis, clustering, and pretrained transformers, but stable subtype replication and clinical actionability remain open.[7] [10] [12] Biological-value evidence is largely cross-sectional or uses estimated risk rather than observed outcomes.[15] [16]

Scoping methods and provenance

Review question and boundaries

1 · Excursion segmentation

Locate meaningful upward CGM events without assigning cause.

2 · Meal-associated inference

Infer likely meal responses from CGM morphology alone; labels may validate but not define inference-time input.

3 · Excursion classification

Segment first, then classify likely meal versus non-meal from CGM alone.

Person phenotype

Aggregate event or daily patterns into recurring signatures or mixtures.

Terminology guardrail. An upward CGM excursion is observed. Meal association is inferred unless a suitable external reference label confirms it. A recorded meal validates timing but cannot be smuggled into a supposedly CGM-only event definition.

01 Study objective and estimands

SPEC. Determine whether externally validated, CGM-only, algorithm-defined glucose excursions provide reproducible, incremental information about metabolic dysfunction beyond A1C and the current global CGM representation (six axes / 52 metrics), specifically for adults without diabetes or with early dysglycemia. The primary science question is about excursion start time extraction and recovery dynamics — not meal-detection headline metrics. A candidate event is an algorithm-defined glucose excursion; meal probability remains externally calibrated and uncertain in AI-READI.

  • Primary estimand: adjusted difference in continuous TG/HDL-C per one-SD higher event-recovery score, conditional on A1C, age, sex, BMI, site, wear quality, and the locked six-axis CGM baseline. Cross-sectional conditional association, not causal.
  • Primary predictive contrast: difference in participant-held-out performance between reference (covariates + A1C + six axes; separate 52-metric regularized reference) and augmented (reference + prespecified event features). The claim is about the difference and its uncertainty.
  • Prespecified hypotheses: H1 recovery dynamics (primary); H2 A1C heterogeneity; H4 circadian recovery; H5 detector disagreement (secondary). Terminology: use metabolic dysfunction and impaired glucose recovery dynamics; do not use "metabolic inflexibility" as a measured endpoint.
